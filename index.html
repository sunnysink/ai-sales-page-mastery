Open your voice memo app or Loom and answer these eight questions about your offer. Talk naturally. The more detail, the better your sales page will be.

✦ Don't overthink it. Pretend you're telling a friend about what you do over coffee. Claude works best with your raw, unfiltered voice. Aim for 5 to 15 minutes total. If you get stuck on any question, just mention it in your recording and ask Claude to make suggestions or fill in the blanks for you.
📌 A heads up: when you get to the questions about what you do and what makes you different, lean into the transformation your client experiences, not the modalities you use to get there. It's okay to mention your tools and training, but your sales page will be strongest when it leads with what shifts for the person sitting across from you.

Your Eight Questions

1

What is your offer and who is it for?

Describe your session, service, or program in your own words. Who is the specific person it's designed to help? Paint a picture of them.

2

What is the core transformation or outcome?

What does your client walk away with? What shifts for them? Describe the before and after. If you can, list 3 to 5 specific outcomes. These will fill the outcomes section of your sales page, so the more specific you are here, the stronger that section will be.

3

What are the 5 to 7 biggest frustrations your ideal client is dealing with right now?

Get specific. What keeps them stuck? What have they already tried that didn't work? What are they sick of hearing?

4

What does your session or service actually include, and how is it delivered?

Walk through what happens step by step. What do they get? Is it over Zoom, in person, Voxer, a portal? How long is it? How many sessions? Describe the full experience from their side.

5

What questions do people ask before they book or buy?

Think about the DMs, the emails, the hesitations. What do they need to know before they say yes?

6

What makes your approach different from everyone else doing something similar?

This is your edge. Your philosophy, your method, your lived experience. What do you bring that nobody else can?

7

What is your method, framework, or signature process called?

If you've named it, tell us. If you haven't yet, describe the steps you take clients through. Walk us through your process from start to finish.

8

Do you have any testimonials or client results to share?

Read them out loud or paraphrase what past clients have said. Include their name if you have permission. If you don't have testimonials yet, just say so. You can choose to skip that section or have Claude write placeholders you'll swap out later.

Host on GitHub Pages

1

Create a GitHub Account

Go to github.com and sign up for a free account if you don't already have one. Confirm your email and you're in.

2

Create a New Repository

Click the + in the top right corner and select "New repository."

Name it something related to your offer (e.g., my-coaching-sales-page). This name becomes part of your URL.

Set it to Public.

Click "Create repository."

3

Upload Your HTML File

On your repository page, click "uploading an existing file" (or the "Add file" dropdown, then "Upload files").

Drag your final HTML file into the upload area. Important: rename your file to index.html before uploading. This tells GitHub it's your main page.

Click "Commit changes."

4

Turn on GitHub Pages

Go to your repository's Settings tab (the gear icon).

In the left sidebar, click "Pages."

Under "Branch," select main from the dropdown. Leave the folder as / (root).

Click "Save."

5

Grab Your Live URL

Give it a minute or two. Refresh the Pages settings page and your live URL will appear at the top:

https://yourusername.github.io/my-coaching-sales-page
✦ That URL is your live sales page. Link it from your Linktree, email signature, Instagram bio, Skool community, checkout page... anywhere. To update the page later, just upload a new index.html file to the same repository and it'll update automatically.

Your sales page is live. ✦
